Applies to Stravik Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum batteries: SMF, EFB, AGM, commercial heavy duty, motorcycle and deep cycle.

1. Purpose and scope

This policy is the only warranty offered on Stravik batteries. It sets out what Stravik warrants, how the warranty is activated, how a claim is made and how it is decided. It applies to Stravik distributors, dealers, fitment centres and the end user.

No distributor, dealer, fitment centre or employee may extend, change or override these terms unless confirmed in writing by Zenter Parts. In case of conflict between translations, the English text prevails.

2. Warranty activation: fitment registration

No registration, no warranty

A Stravik battery is warranted only when its fitment has been registered on the Stravik platform (stravikparts.com) within 24 hours of fitment, with all the information and photos listed below.

The party that fits the battery registers it: the distributor, the dealer or the fitment centre. A battery sold over the counter must be fitted and registered within 30 days of sale.

A battery without a complete registration has no warranty.

Zenter Parts accepts no claim on a battery without a complete registration. Any obligation to the end customer on an unregistered battery is carried by the dealer that sold or fitted it.

4. Start date, stock age and replacements

  • The warranty starts on the date of fitment recorded on the platform.
  • A battery must be fitted within 9 months of the batch date printed on the case. A battery fitted later is warranted from batch date plus 9 months: the extra months are deducted from the cover.
  • A replacement battery carries the remaining cover of the original battery, from the original fitment date. It does not restart the warranty. The replacement is registered on the platform in the same way.
  • Example: a battery with 24 months of cover fails at month 8 and is replaced. The replacement battery is covered for the remaining 16 months, so the total cover stays 24 months from the original fitment date.
  • The warranty belongs to the original purchaser and the vehicle recorded at fitment. Moving the battery to another vehicle ends the warranty.

5. What is covered

Stravik warrants that its batteries are free from defects in materials and workmanship under normal use, for the period that applies. Covered defects, confirmed by test:

  • Shorted or dead cell.
  • Open circuit or internal break.
  • Cranking performance below 80% of the CCA printed on the label after a full charge, in the free replacement period.
  • Leak at the case or lid seal, or faulty terminal casting, with no sign of impact or misuse.
  • Deep cycle batteries: less than 50% of rated capacity in a capacity test to 10.5 V after a full charge.

The remedy is replacement of the battery, free or at 50% according to the period, at the decision of Stravik.

7. How to make a claim

  • Return the battery to the point of purchase, or to the fitment centre that fitted it, within 30 days of the defect appearing and before the end of the warranty period.
  • Bring the invoice or the platform registration number, and the vehicle if requested.
  • The dealer tests the battery and the vehicle and decides the claim according to the Stravik battery diagnostic chart.
  • The claim is registered on the Stravik platform with the test photos before any replacement is given.

9. Dealer and fitment centre obligations

  • Test the vehicle before every fitment and refuse to fit when the charging or starting system is outside the limits.
  • Fit only the battery listed for the vehicle in the Stravik application list.
  • Register every fitment on the platform within 24 hours with all information and photos, and give the customer the Stravik warranty card.
  • Explain the warranty period printed on the battery label and the claim procedure to the customer at the sale.
  • Rotate stock first in, first out, check voltage every 3 months and recharge below 12.4 V.
  • Keep test records and photos for at least 24 months.

Dealer liability

A dealer or fitment centre that fails to register a fitment, registers false information, or fits a battery to a vehicle that failed the fitment test, carries the cost of any resulting claim. Zenter Parts will recover that cost from the party responsible.

10. Disputed claims

A customer who disputes a decision may ask for an independent test at a battery testing facility accepted by Zenter Parts, at the customer's cost. Zenter Parts reviews the result and replies in writing within 14 business days. If the independent test confirms a manufacturing defect, the test cost is refunded.

11. General terms

  • This policy is governed by the law of the country where the battery was sold. Nothing in it limits rights the buyer has under the consumer law of that country.
  • In South Africa this policy applies together with the Consumer Protection Act. In the UAE, Saudi Arabia and the GCC it applies in line with local law.
  • Zenter Parts may amend this policy. Amendments apply to batteries fitted from the date of publication; batteries fitted earlier remain under the policy in force at their fitment date.
  • This policy replaces all earlier warranty statements, cards or verbal statements. No oral statement by any distributor, dealer or employee is binding unless confirmed in writing by Zenter Parts.
  • The maximum liability under this warranty is replacement of the defective battery.
