CRITICAL — SHELF STOCK RULE

No warranty claim will be honored if the battery has been in distributor or dealer stock for more than 6 months from the date of manufacture before sale to the end user. Battery date codes are verified against the claim date. FIFO stock rotation is mandatory.

Note for taxi and fleet applications: The 6-month warranty reflects the extreme duty cycle. Proof that the vehicle is used as a taxi or commercial fleet vehicle voids any longer warranty tier regardless of the product label fitted.

Replacement batteries supplied under an accepted warranty claim carry only the remaining balance of the original warranty period from the original purchase date, with a minimum of 90 days. A replacement does not reset or restart the warranty period.

3. What Is Covered

Stravik warrants that its products are free from defects in materials and workmanship under normal use conditions for the duration of the applicable warranty period.

Covered defects include:

  • Internal short circuit resulting from separator failure or manufacturing defect, confirmed by load test with no external cause identified
  • Sudden complete failure within the first weeks of installation with no external cause identified after full diagnostic assessment
  • Demonstrable manufacturing fault confirmed by Stravik technical assessment

Warranty remedy is limited to repair or replacement of the defective battery at Stravik's sole discretion. Stravik does not cover:

  • Transport or freight costs associated with the claim
  • Labor costs for removal or installation
  • Consequential, indirect, or incidental losses of any kind
  • Damage to any other vehicle component or system

LOAD SHEDDING / POWER INSTABILITY: Batteries used with generators, UPS systems, or inverters are not covered under any automotive warranty tier. Permanent sulphation resulting from load-shedding discharge cycles is not a manufacturing defect.

5. Conditions Precedent to Any Claim

The following are non-negotiable prerequisites. Claims submitted without satisfying all conditions will be rejected before technical assessment:

  • Battery must be registered in the Stravik system within 24 hours of fitment by the installing dealer, recording: customer name, vehicle details, battery serial number, and alternator voltage at fitment.
  • Original proof of purchase must be presented. The serial number on the battery must match the warranty certificate.
  • Alternator voltage at fitment must have been recorded on the registration form and must fall within 13.4V–14.4V. Claims where this was not recorded are rejected.
  • The claim must be lodged by the end user as soon as the defect is discovered. Claims must be submitted to an authorized Stravik dealer within 30 days of the defect becoming apparent and before expiry of the warranty period.
  • The battery must be made available for physical inspection and testing at an authorized Stravik facility.

Registration is mandatory, not optional. The 24-hour registration window is the dealer's responsibility.

If a dealer fails to register the battery, the dealer, not Stravik, bears liability for any resulting claim dispute.

Proof of alternator voltage at fitment is the single most powerful fraud-prevention and fault-diagnosis tool in the system. Without it, no claim proceeds.

6. Claim Assessment Procedure

All warranty claims must be assessed using the following 6-step procedure. Steps must be performed in order. A claim may only be approved after all 6 steps are completed with results recorded.

Step

Action

Detail & Pass/Fail Criteria

STEP 1

Visual Inspection & Pre-Qualification

Inspect case, lid, terminals, hold-down evidence. Check proof of purchase and warranty certificate. Check serial number matches certificate. Check battery is within warranty period. Any fail → REJECT immediately. Do not proceed.

STEP 2

Charge Battery to Minimum 75% SoC

Measure OCV and SG. If OCV < 12.0V or SG < 1.200 (tropical), charge at 1–2% CCA rate. Do not load test a discharged battery. Allow electrolyte to stabilize before reading SG.

STEP 3

State-of-Charge Test — Hydrometer

Measure all cells. Maximum acceptable variance between cells: 0.010 SG. Variance > 0.040 SG = faulty cell. Confirm with load test.

STEP 4

Load Test — High Rate Discharge (HRD)

Apply IEC CCA load for 15 seconds. Record final voltage AND needle movement pattern. See diagnostic table. A battery that recovers above 9.6V is not warranty-eligible.

STEP 5

Alternator Voltage Verification

Request customer's alternator voltage at fitment (from registration form). If above 14.8V or below 13.4V: reject claim. If not recorded on registration form: reject claim.

STEP 6

Decision & Documentation

Match test results to diagnostic table. Record all readings. Complete Stravik Warranty Claim Form with all evidence. Submit to authorized distributor within 30 days of becoming aware of the defect.

7. Disputed Claims

If a claimant disputes the outcome of Stravik's warranty assessment, the claimant may commission an independent technical assessment at the claimant's own cost. The independent assessor must be a qualified battery testing facility acceptable to Stravik.

If the independent assessment differs from Stravik's determination, Stravik will review and respond in writing within 14 business days. Stravik reserves the right to reject the independent assessment if it does not meet accepted technical standards.

In all cases, statutory consumer rights under applicable local law remain unaffected. This warranty does not limit any rights the consumer holds at law that cannot be excluded.

8. Authorized Dealer Obligations

Authorized dealers are the first line of warranty integrity. The following obligations are mandatory:

  • Test alternator voltage before every battery fitment. Record on the registration form. Refuse to fit if outside 13.4V–14.4V.
  • Register every battery in the Stravik system within 24 hours of fitment. Provide the customer with the warranty certificate.
  • Explain the claim procedure to the customer at point of sale.
  • Maintain FIFO stock rotation. No battery in stock for more than 6 months without a recharge cycle.
  • Never fit an automotive Stravik battery into a leisure, UPS, solar, or inverter application.
  • Document and retain warranty assessment records for a minimum of 24 months.

DEALER LIABILITY

A dealer who fails to register a battery, fails to record alternator voltage, or fits a battery into a known-faulty electrical system assumes personal liability for any resulting warranty claim. Stravik will seek recovery from the responsible dealer where non-compliance with these obligations is demonstrated.
