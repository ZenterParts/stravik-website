Description

SVPDT021 · Platinum Series, the flagship Stravik range (blue label band).

A sealed maintenance free (SMF) battery with calcium-alloy grids. Water loss is very low, so it never needs topping up, and it holds its charge well when the vehicle is parked. Designed for vehicles without start-stop.

Capacity and starting power

100 Ah capacity (20-hour rate) and 850 A cold cranking current (EN, at −18 °C). Choose values equal to or higher than the original battery.

Case and fitting

L5B is a standard European DIN case (EN 50342-2), in the low 175 mm height for vehicles with limited space under the bonnet. Size 352 × 175 × 175 mm.

Layout 0: with the terminals on the side nearest to you, the positive (+) terminal is on the right.

Hold-down B3: the ledge at the base of the case that the vehicle clamp grips (EN 50342-2). Match it to the original battery so the battery is held firmly.

Before you orderCheck the SAB number, case size, terminal layout and hold-down against the old battery, or send us the vehicle and we'll confirm the fit.
