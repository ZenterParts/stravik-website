Description

SVGHK010 · Gold Series, the Stravik performance range (bronze label band).

A heavy duty battery for trucks, buses, agricultural and construction equipment. Robust plates and case are designed for high cranking loads, constant vibration and long operating hours.

Capacity and starting power

250 Ah capacity (20-hour rate) and 1350 A cold cranking current (EN, at −18 °C). Choose values equal to or higher than the original battery.

Case and fitting

C is a heavy duty case for commercial and off-road vehicles. Size 516 × 275 × 235 mm.

Layout 3: heavy duty layout. Layouts 3 and 4 are mirror images, so compare the positive (+) terminal position with the old battery or the technical drawing.

Hold-down B00: no ledge at the base of the case. The battery is secured by a top frame or clamp.

Before you orderCheck the SAB number, case size, terminal layout and hold-down against the old battery, or send us the vehicle and we'll confirm the fit.
