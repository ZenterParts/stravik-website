Description

SVSDK009 · Silver Series, the Stravik value range (grey label band).

A sealed maintenance free (SMF) battery with calcium-alloy grids. Water loss is very low, so it never needs topping up, and it holds its charge well when the vehicle is parked. Designed for vehicles without start-stop.

Capacity and starting power

50 Ah capacity (20-hour rate) and 540 A cold cranking current (EN, at −18 °C). Choose values equal to or higher than the original battery.

Case and fitting

L2 is a standard European DIN case (EN 50342-2), 190 mm high. Size 244 × 174 × 190 mm.

Layout 1: with the terminals on the side nearest to you, the positive (+) terminal is on the left.

Hold-down B13: the ledge at the base of the case that the vehicle clamp grips (EN 50342-2). Match it to the original battery so the battery is held firmly.

Before you orderCheck the SAB number, case size, terminal layout and hold-down against the old battery, or send us the vehicle and we'll confirm the fit.
