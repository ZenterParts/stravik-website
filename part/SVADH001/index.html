Description

SVADH001 · AGM Start-Stop range of absorbent glass mat batteries.

An absorbent glass mat (AGM) battery. The electrolyte is held in glass-fibre mats, making the battery spill-proof, vibration resistant and capable of the highest cycling performance. Designed for advanced start-stop systems with brake energy recovery.

Capacity and starting power

60 Ah capacity (20-hour rate) and 680 A cold cranking current (EN, at −18 °C). Choose values equal to or higher than the original battery.

Case and fitting

L2 is a standard European DIN case (EN 50342-2), 190 mm high. Size 242 × 175 × 190 mm.

Layout 0: with the terminals on the side nearest to you, the positive (+) terminal is on the right.

Hold-down B13: the ledge at the base of the case that the vehicle clamp grips (EN 50342-2). Match it to the original battery so the battery is held firmly.

Start-stop vehicles: replace like for like (AGM with AGM, EFB with EFB or AGM) and register the new battery with the vehicle's battery management system after fitting.

Before you orderCheck the SAB number, case size, terminal layout and hold-down against the old battery, or send us the vehicle and we'll confirm the fit.
