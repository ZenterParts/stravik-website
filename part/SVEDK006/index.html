Description

SVEDK006 · EFB-II Start-Stop range of enhanced flooded batteries.

An enhanced flooded battery (EFB) built for start-stop vehicles. Reinforced plates and carbon additives give fast charge acceptance and a much higher cycle life than a standard battery, for reliable engine restarts in traffic.

Capacity and starting power

80 Ah capacity (20-hour rate) and 830 A cold cranking current (EN, at −18 °C). Choose values equal to or higher than the original battery.

Case and fitting

L4 is a standard European DIN case (EN 50342-2), 190 mm high. Size 310 × 174 × 190 mm.

Layout 1: with the terminals on the side nearest to you, the positive (+) terminal is on the left.

Hold-down B13: the ledge at the base of the case that the vehicle clamp grips (EN 50342-2). Match it to the original battery so the battery is held firmly.

Start-stop vehicles: replace like for like (AGM with AGM, EFB with EFB or AGM) and register the new battery with the vehicle's battery management system after fitting.

Before you orderCheck the SAB number, case size, terminal layout and hold-down against the old battery, or send us the vehicle and we'll confirm the fit.
