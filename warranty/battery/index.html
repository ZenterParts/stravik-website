Fitment test

Test after fitting the battery, engine warm and loads off, with a battery and system tester with EFB, AGM and start-stop modes, used to the tester maker's procedure. A voltmeter is accepted only on vehicles without start-stop or smart charging. Photograph each reading on the tester or voltmeter screen. Outside any limit: do not fit.
